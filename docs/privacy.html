Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ALT Marketing LLC ("we") operates an internal tool that copies videos our own creators publish on Instagram to the same creators' TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What we access

With the account owner's permission, we access: the list of videos on their Instagram account (video file, caption, post date); and permission to upload videos to their TikTok and YouTube accounts. We also receive basic profile info (such as display name) from TikTok.

How we use it

Only to republish the account owner's own videos to their own accounts. We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising.

Storage

Login tokens are stored encrypted. Video files are deleted right after upload. We keep a list of which videos were already republished so nothing is posted twice.

Removing access

Account owners can revoke access at any time in their Instagram, TikTok, or Google account settings, or by contacting us at the email below.
